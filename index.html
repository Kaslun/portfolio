— About

I design interactive systems that respond to humans. That's the through-line under everything: gameplay programming on award-winning games at Hyper Games, training simulations and dialogue logic at Attensi, AI agents that have to feel like specific people.

The path looks linear in hindsight, but each step felt like a side step at the time. What connects them isn't the technology. It's the question. How do you make a system feel alive to the person interacting with it?
